title:          "TESGNN: Temporal equivariant scene graph neural networks for efficient and robust multi-view 3d scene understanding"
date:           2025-07-24 00:01:00 +0800
selected:       true
pub:            "Transactions on Machine Learning Research TMLR 2025"
abstract: >-
we propose TESGNN, consisting of two key components (1) an Equivariant Scene Graph Neural Network that extracts information from 3D point clouds to generate scene graph while preserving crucial symmetry properties, and (2) a Temporal Graph Matching that fuses local scene graphs across multiple time sequences into a unified global representation. TESGNN shown to be effective compared to existing methods in scene graph generation, produces a more stable and accurate global scene representation compared to existing approaches.
